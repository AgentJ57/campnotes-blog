About Camp Notes

Built around
the fire.

The best advice we ever got about life on the road did not come from a travel magazine. It came from a stranger at a campground who just told us what actually worked.

There is a thing that happens around campfires that does not happen anywhere else. People tell the truth. Not the polished version, not the Instagram version — the version where they admit what they got wrong, what they wish they had known, and what they would do differently.

That is what Camp Notes is trying to be on the internet.

Every article we publish is built around one question: does this actually help someone make a better decision on the road? If the answer is no, we don't publish it.

We cover campground memberships, RV gear, free camping, road costs, and family travel — not because those are popular categories, but because those are the areas where bad information is most expensive. A bad membership recommendation costs you hundreds of dollars. A bad gear recommendation fails you at 11pm in a campground with no water pressure.

We take that seriously. Every guide is built around real data, real products, and real experience. When there is an affiliate relationship, we say so. When a product does not hold up, we say that too.

What this site stands for

🔥
Honest over polished
We would rather publish something that admits uncertainty than something that sounds confident and turns out wrong.
🌲
Specific over vague
Real numbers, real campgrounds, real break-even math. Not "it depends" dressed up as advice.
⛺
Useful over impressive
Every piece of content should leave you knowing something you can actually use. If it doesn't, it shouldn't be here.
🗺️
Transparent about money
This site earns through affiliate links and display advertising. Every relationship is disclosed. That is how we stay free.

Why you can trust what we publish

01
Experience behind the content
The guides draw from six years of full-time road travel across 48 states, real memberships, and gear tested in actual conditions.
02
Affiliate relationships disclosed

03

04
We only recommend what we would use
If a product doesn't hold up to honest scrutiny, it doesn't get a guide. Recommending bad products to earn a commission kills trust. That's not a trade we're willing to make.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Camp Notes collects, how it is used, and what rights you have over it.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Camp Notes is a travel and camping blog located at campnotes.blog. For questions about this policy, contact us at contact@campnotes.blog.

What data we collect

We collect the following types of data:

  • Email addresses and first names — when you subscribe to our weekly email list through any form on this site.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, and general location data collected automatically through Google Analytics.
  • Cookie data — see the Cookie Policy section below.

We do not collect payment information. We do not collect sensitive personal data.

How we use your data

  • Email addresses are used solely to send our weekly newsletter. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party.
  • Usage data is used to understand how readers use the site so we can improve our content.
  • We never use your data to make automated decisions that affect you.

Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. Camp Notes uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality, including remembering your cookie preferences.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data. You can opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Advertising cookies — if you have accepted cookies, Google AdSense may place cookies to show you relevant ads. Google's privacy policy applies to this data: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Affiliate cookies — affiliate partners may place cookies when you click their links. These cookies help partners track referrals and attribute commissions. We do not control these cookies.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Affiliate disclosure

Camp Notes participates in affiliate marketing programs. Some links on this site may earn a small commission if you click and make a purchase. The price you pay is never affected by this. All affiliate relationships are disclosed at the top of posts and in the site footer.

Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time (for example, by unsubscribing from emails)
  • The right to data portability
  • California residents: we do not sell personal information as defined under CCPA

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@campnotes.blog.

Data retention

We retain email subscriber data for as long as you remain subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your data is deleted within 30 days. Analytics data is retained for 26 months per Google Analytics default settings.

Third party services

This site uses the following third party services that may process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdSense — policies.google.com/privacy

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
